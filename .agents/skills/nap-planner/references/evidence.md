# Evidence and source boundaries

No app-specific research bundle is included in this skill. Do not claim otherwise. When integrating additional research, read the original material and record the source, claims actually supported, and methodological limits here before changing planning rules. Keep private child histories and app credentials outside the reusable skill.

Primary sources checked on 2026-11-08:

- [AASM pediatric sleep duration consensus](https://aasm.org/resources/pdf/pressroom/pediatric-sleep-duration-consensus.pdf): broad recommendations for regular total sleep over 24 hours, including naps; these are not per-nap targets or a personalized wake-window model. The statement does not set a duration recommendation for infants younger than four months.
- [AAP safe sleep guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/sleep/Pages/a-parents-guide-to-safe-sleep.aspx): infants should be placed on their backs on a firm, flat sleep surface. Apply safe sleep guidance to naps as well as nighttime. Do not optimize by recommending unsafe sleep arrangements.

This version contains no validated developmental forecasting model, personalized sleep-pressure model, or clinical wake-window thresholds. Solver bounds are explicit parent-informed planning assumptions. Neither these sources nor the arithmetic solver establish that extending wake windows causes better naps.

When adding medical or developmental claims, verify them against current primary guidance and cite the exact supporting source. Keep general guidance distinct from the child's clinician instructions and parent-reported patterns. Use a brief relevant explanation when safety affects the proposed plan; avoid repeating generic warnings in every response.
